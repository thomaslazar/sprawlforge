# Arterials Phase 3 (Half Interchanges) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An arterial that has nowhere to go past the highway ends on it as a T instead of being pruned back, a T end gets two ramps on its side (a half interchange) when they fit, and a quarter of the plain square crossings get a half interchange by chance.

**Architecture:** The T end is cut in the §3.4 end pass (`joinFreeEnds`, join.ts), in one sweep after the join passes, and `pruneDangling` gets one hook so the arterial prune treats an end on the highway as anchored. `highwayCrossings` finds T ends by geometry (an arterial end on the centreline), so nothing new is carried out of `traceRoads`. `buildInterchanges` adds half interchanges after the full ones, using its existing `rampTriples` with only one arterial side. Render is unchanged: ramps are roads.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, vitest, Playwright uicheck (`PORT=4174 tools/uicheck/run.sh`).

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-12-arterials-design.md` §6 (phase 3), with §3.4, §7 (the "Phase 3:", re-pin and uicheck lines), §8, §9. Phases 1 and 2 are merged (main 62199f8).

Decisions this plan pins (the spec leaves them open):
- **Where the cut happens:** inside `joinFreeEnds`, as one sweep after the three join passes, over every end still free by the same test the join loop uses. An end the join passes could still reach (order-dependent joins) is joined first; a cut only ever removes geometry, so nothing after it needs a re-run.
- **A tail with a junction is not cut.** If the stretch from the crossing to the end meets another arterial (within 6 m, more than 6 m past the crossing), the end stays free: the prune cuts back to that junction, which keeps more road than a cut at the highway. The spec's "last crossing" is read from the end: the crossing nearest the end along the road.
- **Prune hook:** `PruneOpts.anchorEnd?: (p: Pt) => boolean` (the spec's "or equivalent"). An end for which it returns true counts as anchored. The arterial prune passes `(p) => !!highway && distToPolyline(p, highway.points) <= 1`. `accept` stays `c === 'arterial'`, so the highway still anchors nothing else (no interior junction, no inward walk).
- **Carrying T ends:** by geometry, not state. `highwayCrossings` adds `{ roadId, at, kind, interchange: false, tee: true }` for each arterial end within `TEE_SNAP_M = 2` m of the centreline (the 1 m T-end tolerance plus the 1 m `simplify` that `traceRoads` applies to the highway afterwards). `HighwayCrossing` gains `tee?: boolean` and `half?: boolean`.
- **Tee kind (the plain T's look):** elevated → `'under'`, sunken → `'over'`, ground → `'under'`, with no rng draw. The renderer already draws half the over/under piece at a road end (`slicePolyline` clamps at 1), so a plain T on an elevated or sunken highway is the spec's under/over-pass stub with no new SVG.
- **Ground-level highways:** no special case. A T end on a ground stretch is a T end like any other (arterials already cross ground stretches grade-separated today); `cutStreetsAtGround` is streets-only and unchanged.
- **Half interchange eligibility:** tees, in highway order: `rampTriples(c, [side])` fits, where `side` is the arterial's side (`-1` when the end is the arterial's last point, `+1` when it is the first), plus the same not-on-water and 200 m transition clearance as full interchanges, and ≥ `IC_REACH_M` from every full and every half chosen so far. Chance halves, afterwards, in highway order, over non-tee non-interchange arterial crossings that pass the full rule's `squareEnough` (≥ 45°) and `continues` (both sides ≥ `IC_CONTINUE_M` = 300 m or meeting another arterial), the water and transition checks and the spacing: draw `rng.chance(HALF_CHANCE)`; on a hit draw the side `rng.chance(0.5) ? -1 : 1`; if that side's ramps do not fit, no half (no second try).
- **Ramp ids:** full interchanges' ramps first, exactly as today (ids unchanged), then each half's two ramps in the order the halves were chosen (tees, then chance), continuing `R<n>`.
- **RNG plumbing:** `buildInterchanges(…, sizeM, halfRng: Rng)`, required. The existing unit tests pass a `never` rng (`chance: () => false`), so they keep testing the full rule alone.

- Produces (join.ts): `export const TEE_MIN_M = 300`, `export const TEE_MIN_ANGLE = Math.PI / 4`, `export const TEE_SNAP_M = 2` (used by Task 3 and the tests). `joinFreeEnds` keeps its signature and return type.

The tee sweep, after the pass loop, for each non-ring arterial end that is still free (same skip test as the join loop: not on the window edge, not within 15 m of water, no other arterial within 6 m; also skip an end already within 1 m of a highway):
1. Walk the segments from the end inward; the first `index.firstCrossing(fwd[i+1], fwd[i], (_id, c) => c === 'highway')` hit is the crossing nearest the end. None → stays free.
2. Angle: `angleGapLines(atan2 of fwd[i]→fwd[i+1], hit.segAngle) >= TEE_MIN_ANGLE`, else free.
3. Kept road `[...fwd.slice(0, i + 1), hit.at]` (drop `fwd[i]` if within 1 m of `hit.at`) must be ≥ `TEE_MIN_M`, else free.
4. The tail (from `hit.at` to the end, 5 m samples, those more than 6 m of arc past the crossing) must have no other arterial within 6 m, else free.
5. Cut: replace the road's points (reverse back if `atStart`), `index.remove` / `index.add`, `out[ri]` as the join does. No re-smoothing (a cut of a smoothed road).

`traceRoads`: the arterial `pruneDangling` gets `anchorEnd: (p) => !!highway && distToPolyline(p, highway.points) <= 1` (`distToPolyline` from `../terrain/rivers`). The comment becomes "an arterial ends on an arterial / water / the edge / the highway (a T end, §6.1), or the tail is cut".
